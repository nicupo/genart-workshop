# Workshop

Audience:
Programmers with little or no experience in generative art.

Duration:
3h online + 4h in person.

Goal:
Teach generative art as designing systems rather than drawing images.

Requirements:
- p5.js
- Interactive
- Cellular automata
- Reaction diffusion
- Sound (optional)
- Arduino examples (optional)
- Final collective piece

Important:
Not with / about AI-generated art.
Focus on experimentation and play.

Prefer references to:
- Vera Molnár
- Casey Reas
- Karl Sims
- Jared Tarbell
- Daniel Shiffman
- academic sources
